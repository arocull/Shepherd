Shepherd, a humble game prototype by StagMath (Alan O'Cull)

Controls:
- WASD or Arrow Keys to move (may need to hold)
- Spacebar to swing your staff (performs various actions)
- Your character always faces the direction you last moved in
- Escape to open pause menu
	- Tap W/up or S/down to navigate
	- D/right to select
- F11 to toggle fullscreen

Notes:
- There must be a "save" directory in the folder you run the game executable from, even if it's empty. It will not save otherwise.
- This game has no framerate controls, so you may want to keep it in windowed mode if your monitor is over 60Hz, to prevent slowdowns.
- I've only tested this on Ubuntu and Debian-based Linux installs. I cannot guarantee functionality elsewhere.

Rambles:
Thanks for choosing to try out Shepherd!

I made this game with very limited tools during the awkward transition between my senior year in highschool
(during which the COVID-19 epidemic started in the USA), and my freshman year at college.

This game was my intro to Linux, and I considered any time I spent making it as a time of worship with the Lord.
The game itself could stand for improvement (to this day my level designs are still very mid), but the journey itself is what's important to me now.

The game ends when you defeat the Golem boss at the end of the Pyramid.
This game also had sheep-eating wolves and freezing liquids, but they never made it into my initial level designs.
The levels are easy to mess with though (see Assets/Maps/) so feel free to try messing with them!

Thank you so much again for playing!
